Reading the results

Visual captures without approved references are marked unreviewed. Performance measurements are informational while calibration is in progress. Missing, failed and non-comparable results remain explicit.

These checks use simulated devices. The recorded scenarios define the evidence available; they do not establish compatibility with every installation.
